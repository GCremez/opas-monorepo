# Content rating (IARC)

Play Console → App content → Content rating asks the IARC questionnaire, then
issues a rating per region. Opas is a utility that explains the user's own
letters; it has no game content, no social feed and no advertising. Every answer
below is **No** unless the table says otherwise, which produces the lowest
rating: **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / IARC 3+**.

## Questionnaire answers

| Section | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Violence | Does the app contain violence? | No |
| Violence | Blood, injury or death? | No |
| Violence | Sexual violence? | No |
| Sexuality | Nudity or sexual content? | No |
| Sexuality | Suggestive content? | No |
| Language | Profanity or crude humour? | No |
| Controlled substances | Drugs, alcohol or tobacco? | No |
| Gambling | Simulated or real gambling? | No |
| Fear | Content that may frighten or disturb? | No |
| User-generated content | Can users create or publish content other people can see? | No |
| User-generated content | Can users interact or communicate with each other? | No |
| User-generated content | Can users share their location? | No |
| User-generated content | Can users share personal information with other users? | No |
| Purchases | Does the app contain digital purchases? | **No** — `CAN_PURCHASE` is false on Android; subscriptions are bought on the website, outside the app |
| Advertising | Does the app contain ads? | No |
| Miscellaneous | Does the app contain any other content that may require a higher rating? | No |

### Why "user-generated content" is No

The app handles letters and photos, but they are private to the account. There is
no feed, no sharing link to another user, no comments, and no way to see anyone
else's content. `/d/<uuid>` is a personal deep link that requires the owner's
session; it does not publish the letter.

### Why "digital purchases" is No

There is no checkout in the Android build. `src/lib/billing.ts` exports
`CAN_PURCHASE = false`, and the subscription screen explains that plans are
managed on the website. When Play Billing lands (P1-6), change this answer to
**Yes** and rerun the questionnaire; the rating usually stays the same, but the
declaration must match the build.

## Expected result

| Region | Rating |
|---|---|
| Europe (PEGI) | PEGI 3 |
| United States (ESRB) | Everyone |
| Germany (USK) | USK 0 |
| Brazil (ClassInd) | L |
| Australia | G |
| Rest of world | IARC 3+ |

Play shows the rating on the listing and may add content descriptors. Nothing in
this app should add one.

## Separate declarations that are not part of IARC

These sit on the same App content page and are easy to confuse with the rating:

- **AI-generated content:** answer **Yes**. The explanations are generated by
  Claude. The policy requires an in-app way to report offensive output; that
  control (P1-5) is not built yet, so this is a production-review blocker even
  though the rating is unaffected.
- **Government app:** answer **No**. Opas is independent and says so in the
  listing, the app and the privacy policy.
- **News app:** No.
- **COVID-19 contact tracing:** No.
- **Data safety:** separate form, see `data-safety.md`.
- **Target audience:** 18+, or 13+ if Play insists on a band. Do not select an
  audience under 13: the app handles health data and official letters, and
  selecting children would trigger the Families policy.

## When to redo this

Rerun the questionnaire if any of these change:

- Play Billing or in-app purchases are added;
- ads or a third-party ad SDK are added;
- users can share or publish a letter;
- a community or chat feature is added;
- the AI output can be reported and that changes the declared content (the
  declaration changes, the rating normally does not).
