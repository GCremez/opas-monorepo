B — Ring Path

The same story told around a continuous circular track.

Three waypoints on a dashed circular ring. The dashed track is the uncertain path; as the eye follows it clockwise, a solid green segment lights up where guidance takes over, ending at the third node with an arrival arrow. The outer ring frames everything without competing.

Stronger geometric precision than Arc Path — feels more "system" and less organic. The dashed-to-solid transition on the same circle creates a clear before/after reading.

C — Node Path

Two nodes still searching, one node arrived. The before-and-after story.

Three positions in an L-shaped path: two grey nodes (the before — uncertainty, searching), one accent-green node with an arrival arrow (the after — guided, arrived). The path itself transitions from dashed (start→middle, still unsure) to solid (middle→end, clarity revealed).

Clearest narrative read of the three variants — you can "read" the story left to right, top to bottom. Two greys to one green literally shows: you're not alone, the system has already guided others.
