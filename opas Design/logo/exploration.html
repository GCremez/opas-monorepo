Compass O

The letter O contains a compass — guidance at the core of the name.

The outer circle is the letter O of Opas. Inside, a two-tone compass needle points NE (45°), suggesting forward motion and clarity. Only the north and east arms are lit — the south and west recede into the background. The mark says: we show you which way to go.

Strongest name integration. The O-as-compass is ownable. Works as both logomark and app icon.

The Guide Mark

A compass rose where only one direction is illuminated — the way forward.

Simplified 4-point compass. Three arms fade into dark grey; one arm (NE) is lit in the accent green. A small dot anchors the centre. The metaphor: we cut through the noise to show you the one direction that matters. Clean, tech-luxury, memorable.

Best app-icon candidate. Strong silhouette at small sizes. The asymmetry (one lit arm) is distinctive and ownable.

Pathfinder

A journey in three steps — from unknown to guided to destination.

Three nodes on a line represent the user's journey: the first is dark (lost/before), the second is accent-green (guidance engaged), the third is accent-green with an arrow (arrival). The first segment is dashed (uncertainty), the second is solid (clarity). Minimal, sequential, hopeful.

Best narrative fit. Tells the story of the product's value in one glance. Unusual — no other AI app uses a path metaphor this cleanly.

North Star

A document-like square with a compass needle — guiding through paperwork.

A rounded square (document/app icon shape) frames a single compass needle pointing true north. The needle's upper half is lit accent green; the lower half is white. Clean duality: the container is the document, the needle is the guidance. Together they say: we guide you through every document.

Strongest product-story fit. The document shape + compass needle visually encodes the entire product value in one symbol. Works perfectly as an app icon.
