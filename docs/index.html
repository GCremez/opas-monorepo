Questions about Finnish letters.

How do I read a Kela letter in English?

Scan the letter with Opas, or add the PDF from Suomi.fi Messages. In about ten seconds you get the letter in plain English: what Kela decided, the amount, the dates, and anything you have to do. The Finnish original stays one tap away.

Can Opas explain letters from Vero and Migri too?

Yes. Opas reads official letters from Verohallinto (the Finnish Tax Administration), Migri (the Finnish Immigration Service), Kela, DVV, employment services and your city, in Finnish or Swedish. That includes residual tax decisions (jäännösvero), requests for more documents (lisäselvityspyyntö) and residence permit decisions.

Is it safe to scan letters with my personal details?

Opas removes your name, personal identity code (henkilötunnus), address and account numbers before anything is read. The masked text is then explained by an AI service, which may process it outside the EU, for example in the United States. Your letters are stored in the EU, and nobody trains AI on them. The privacy policy names every service Opas uses.

Why not translate the letter with ChatGPT or Google Translate?

A translator gives you the words. Opas hides your personal details first, explains what the letter means for you, pulls out every deadline and action, and keeps related letters together, so you don't have to rebuild that by hand each time.

Does Opas work with Suomi.fi Messages?

Yes. Save the PDF or take a screenshot of the message in Suomi.fi, then add it to Opas. It is read and filed just like a paper letter.

What if Opas gets something wrong?

Opas explains letters with AI, and AI can make mistakes. The original letter is always one tap away. For a decision that matters, check the original or ask the office that sent it. Opas doesn't give legal advice.

What's free, and what's in Premium?

Free covers 3 letters a month with plain-English explanations, your dates and actions in Alerts, and files by authority. Premium adds unlimited letters, explanations in Suomi and Svenska too, reminders 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before a deadline, reply drafts in formal Finnish, and export.

Can I delete my letters?

Yes. Delete a single letter, or your whole record and account, from the app at any time.
