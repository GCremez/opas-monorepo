The brown envelope

It arrives in Finnish, from an office you've never heard of. Somewhere inside is a deadline you can't find, a sum you're not sure you owe, and an instruction you can't afford to get wrong. Opas reads it for you, and hands back a short, plain list of what to do, and by when.

How it works

From envelope to done, in three steps.

No forms, no copying text into a translator, no guessing which line matters.

  1. 01 · SCAN

    Photograph the letter

    Point your phone at any official letter, or import a PDF or a photo you already took. Opas reads every line, down to the small print.

  2. 02 · UNDERSTAND

    Opas explains it

    First it removes your name, personal identity code and address. Then it explains the letter in plain language and lifts out the amounts, dates and reference numbers.

  3. 03 · ACT

    Do it in time

    Every due date and task lands in your Needs you list with a reminder. The letter is filed under the office that sent it, ready when someone asks for it.

What you get

Everything a confusing letter needs, in one calm place.

  • Plain-language summaries

    Short, direct sentences instead of officialese, with the original letter always one tap away so you can check it yourself.

  • Deadlines you can't miss

    Opas finds the real due date buried in the text, counts the days you have left, and reminds you in time. Only the reminders you need.

  • Your personal record

    Every letter is filed under the office that sent it and kept searchable, for the day someone asks whether you still have that decision.

  • Your details out first

    Names, ID numbers and addresses are removed before the AI reads a letter. We ask before your first scan. How we handle your data.

Questions

Before you try it.

The short answers. More in Support.

Is Opas from Kela, Vero or Migri?

No. Opas is an independent app and is not connected to any Finnish authority. They have not approved it.

Who sees my letters?

Opas removes names, ID numbers and addresses first. The rest goes to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which may process it outside the EU, for example in the United States. Nobody trains AI on your letters.

Can I trust the explanation?

Use it to understand the letter, then check dates and amounts against the original, which is always one tap away. AI can make mistakes. The letter is what counts.

When can I get it?

Opas is in early testing on Android. Email us the address you use on Google Play and we'll add you to the test. iPhone comes later.
