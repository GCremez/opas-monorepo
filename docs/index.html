The brown envelope

It arrives in Finnish, from an office you've never heard of. Somewhere inside is a deadline you can't find, a sum you're not sure you owe, and an instruction you can't afford to get wrong. Opas reads it for you, and hands back a short, plain list of what to do, and by when.

From envelope to done, in three steps.

01 · SCAN

Photograph the letter

Point your phone at any official letter, or pick a photo you already took. Finnish or Swedish, Opas reads every line, down to the small print.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Opas explains it

First it removes your name, personal identity code and address. Then it explains the letter in plain language and lifts out the facts that matter.

03 · ACT

Never miss the deadline

Every due date and task gets a reminder: a week before, the day before, and on the morning it's due. The letter is filed, ready when you need it.

Everything a confusing letter needs, in one calm place.

◆ EXPLAIN

Plain-language summaries

No jargon used to explain jargon. Short, direct sentences in English, Suomi or Svenska, with the original letter always one tap away, so you can check it yourself.

◆ DEADLINES

Reminders that matter

Opas finds the real due date buried in the text, counts the days you have left, and reminds you in time. Only the reminders you need, never a wall of notifications.

◆ ORGANISE

Your personal record

Every letter is filed under the office that sent it and kept searchable, for the day someone asks whether you still have that decision.

◆ PRIVATE

Your details out first

Names, ID numbers and addresses are removed before the AI reads a letter. We ask before your first scan, and you can delete anything at any time. How we handle your data.
