How can we help?

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

Which letters can Opas read?

Letters in Finnish or Swedish from Kela, Verohallinto, Migri, DVV, TE-palvelut and most other senders. Paper letters and PDFs both work.

Opas explains them in English, Suomi or Svenska, whichever you choose.

Is Opas from Kela, Vero or Migri?

No. Opas is an independent app and is not connected to any Finnish authority. For official information, visit kela.fi, vero.fi or migri.fi.

Who sees my letters?

Opas removes names, ID numbers and addresses first. Then the text goes to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which may process it outside the EU, for example in the United States. Anthropic doesn't train AI on it. More in our Privacy policy.

Can I trust the explanation?

Use it to understand the letter, then check dates and amounts against the original, which is one tap away. The AI can make mistakes. The letter is what counts.

If something looks wrong, email us what happened and which authority the letter was from, without attaching the letter.

Opas couldn't read my photo.

Lay the letter flat, avoid shiny light on the paper, and keep all four corners in the frame. You can also import a PDF.

How do reminders work?

When Opas finds a deadline, it adds it to your Needs you list. With Premium, Opas also reminds you a week, 3 days and 1 day before it's due, and on the morning itself. During the early test everyone gets reminders. Turn on notifications for Opas in your phone settings to get them.

How many letters can I scan?

3 a month on a free account. You get 3 new scans on the 1st of every month.

How do I stop the AI reading my letters?

In the app, open You and turn off AI reading. The next letter you scan asks for your permission again. You can also email privacy@getopas.app and we'll stop reading new letters for your account. Letters already read stay until you delete them.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap You, then Delete Account. No access to the app? Delete it by email.
