Help

How can we help?

Something not working, or did Opas get a letter wrong? Email privacy@getopas.app. Tell us what happened and which authority the letter was from. Please don't attach the letter itself.

Common questions

Is Opas from Kela, Vero or Migri?

No. Opas is an independent app and is not connected to any Finnish authority. For official information, visit kela.fi, vero.fi or migri.fi.

Can I trust the explanation?

Use it to understand the letter, then check dates and amounts against the original. The AI can make mistakes. The letter is what counts.

Who sees my letters?

The text goes to Anthropic's Claude AI, which writes your explanation. Before that, Opas removes names, ID numbers and addresses. More in the Privacy policy.

Opas couldn't read my photo.

Lay the letter flat, avoid shiny light on the paper, and keep all four corners in the frame.

How many letters can I scan?

5 a month on a free account. You get 5 new scans on the 1st of every month.

How do I stop the AI reading my letters?

In the app, go to Profile → AI reading and turn it off.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. No access to the app? Delete it by email.
