Privacy policy

Your letters are private. Here's exactly what happens to them.

To explain your letters, Opas has to read them. We use them for that and nothing else. We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

Salau Distributions (Y-tunnus 3244824-8), Finland, is responsible for your data (the "data controller" in the GDPR). Questions about your data: privacy@getopas.app.

Opas is an independent app. It is not run by, or connected to, Kela, Vero (the Finnish Tax Administration), Migri or any other Finnish authority.

What we collect, and why

The GDPR says we need a reason, called a legal basis, for every kind of data. We use three:

  • Your account: we need it to give you the service.
  • Your consent: you said yes, and you can take it back.
  • Legitimate interest: keeping the app working, in ways you would expect.
  • Explicit consent: for health and other sensitive information in your letters (see below).

How the AI reads your letters

Opas sends the text of your letter to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI. It writes the explanation you see. Anthropic works for us here and does not use your letters to train its AI.

Before sending, Opas removes names, ID numbers, addresses and phone numbers. We may not catch every one, so some personal details can still be in the text.

We ask your permission once, before the first scan. To take it back, go to Profile → AI reading in the app. Opas then stops reading new letters. Letters it has already read stay as they are until you delete them.

Health and other sensitive information

Some letters show sensitive things. A Kela decision can mention an illness or a disability, for example. The GDPR calls this "special category" data and protects it more strictly.

Opas only processes it to explain your letter, and only with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). The consent screen before your first scan names health information, and tapping Agree is that consent. We save the date and the version of the wording you agreed to, so we can show what you saw. If the wording changes, we ask you again.

Opas doesn't use this information for anything else, doesn't build a profile from it, and never shares it except with the services listed below that process your letters for us. Taking back your consent in Profile → AI reading stops all new processing.

How long we keep it

  • Letters and your account: until you delete them. Deleting a letter removes the file and its explanation.
  • Deleting your account removes everything: account, letters, files, explanations, folders, tasks and notification tokens.
  • Sign-in and reset codes expire by themselves.
  • Usage events and crash reports hold no letter content and are deleted after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, give you a copy, or delete it. You can also object to what we do on legitimate interest, and take back your consent. Email privacy@getopas.app. We answer within one month.

You can delete your account in the app, or without the app.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman of Finland: tietosuoja.fi.

How we keep it safe

Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your files are in private storage that only Opas can reach, and download links stop working after an hour. Your sign-in stays in your phone's secure storage.

Children

Opas is made for adults handling their own official letters. It is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies.
