What Opas does

You scan an official letter. Opas tells you in plain language what it says, what you need to do, and by when. It keeps your letters in order and reminds you before deadlines.

Opas is independent

Opas is not run by, or connected to, Kela, Vero (the Finnish Tax Administration), Migri or any other Finnish authority. They have not approved it. For official information, visit kela.fi, vero.fi or migri.fi.

Always check the original letter

The explanations and deadlines come from AI, and AI can be wrong or miss something. Use Opas to understand a letter, not as legal, tax or immigration advice. Before an important decision, check the letter itself and, if you're unsure, contact the authority or an adviser.
