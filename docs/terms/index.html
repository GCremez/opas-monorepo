What Opas does

You scan an official letter. Opas tells you in plain language what it says, what you need to do, and by when. It keeps your letters in order and reminds you before deadlines.

These terms are an agreement between you and Salau Distributions (Y-tunnus 3244824-8), Finland ("we"), who make Opas. You accept them when you create an account.

Opas is independent

Opas is not run by, or connected to, Kela, Vero (the Finnish Tax Administration), Migri or any other Finnish authority. They have not approved it. For official information, visit kela.fi, vero.fi or migri.fi.

Always check the original letter

Opas is not legal, tax or immigration advice. The explanations and deadlines come from AI, and AI can be wrong or miss something. Use Opas to understand a letter. Before an important decision, check the letter itself, which is one tap away in the app, and if you're unsure, contact the authority or an adviser. You remain responsible for your own deadlines.

Your account

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • Use your real email address and keep your password to yourself.
  • Only scan letters that are yours, or that you have the right to handle.
  • Don't try to break or overload Opas, or use it on other people's letters without their permission.

Free and paid use

A free account includes 5 scans a month. If we offer paid plans, you'll see the price, how often you pay, and how to cancel before you buy. Payments go through your app store.

Your letters

Your letters stay yours. We only store and read them to give you the service, as the Privacy policy explains. You can delete a letter, or your whole account, at any time.

If something goes wrong

We work hard to keep Opas running, but we can't promise it will never be down or never make a mistake. We may change or remove features. If we change these terms in a way that matters to you, we'll tell you in the app first.

Sometimes people rely on an explanation without checking the letter, and lose out, for example by missing a deadline. As far as Finnish law allows, we are not responsible for those losses. Your rights as a consumer under Finnish and EU law always apply.

Leaving

You can stop using Opas and delete your account whenever you like. We may close accounts that break these terms, and we'll tell you why.

Law and disagreements

Finnish law applies. If we disagree, please write to us first at privacy@getopas.app. If we can't solve it together, you can contact the Consumer Disputes Board of Finland at kuluttajariita.fi.
