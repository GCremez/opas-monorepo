Delete your account.

You can delete your Opas account, and every letter in it, at any time. There are two ways.

Opas, by Salau Distributions

The short version

  • In the app: You → Delete Account. It's immediate.
  • No access to the app? Email privacy@getopas.app.
  • Everything goes: account, letters, photos, explanations and reminders.
  • Want to keep your account? Delete single letters instead.
In the app By email What gets deleted What stays a while Removing some letters

In the app: right away

  1. Open Opas and tap You, your profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Everything is deleted at once.

Without the app: by email

Send an email to privacy@getopas.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Opas account.

We'll reply to confirm, and delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password and settings
  • Every letter and photo you scanned, and their explanations
  • Your folders, deadlines, tasks and draft letters
  • The notification tokens for your phones

What stays for a while

Usage events and crash reports. They have a random ID but no name, email or letter text, and are deleted after 12 months. If you ever paid for a plan, we keep the payment records that accounting law requires, for as long as it requires.

Only want to remove some letters?

Open the letter in the app and tap Delete. Your account and other letters stay.
